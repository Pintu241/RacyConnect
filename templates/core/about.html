The Systemic Problem

Why RecyConnect Was Founded

In rapidly growing urban economies, over 80% of post-consumer waste recovery is driven by the informal sector: independent community scrap collectors. Despite their vital role in preventing landfill crises, they have historically faced price gouging, lack of formal finance, and social marginalization.

Concurrently, households and commercial facilities suffer from irregular scrap pickup schedules, rigged mechanical spring scales, and zero visibility into genuine market values.

RecyConnect was engineered to organize and digitize this value chain into an audit-ready, dignified circular ecosystem where households earn top value and materials never enter landfills.
