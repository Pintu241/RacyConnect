How RecyConnect Works

A fully digitized end-to-end workflow from household doorstep to industrial recovery plant.

1
Schedule Pickup

Select scrap categories, estimate kilograms, choose your preferred doorstep date/time window, and attach an optional photo.

2
Verified Arrival

A background-checked local collector arrives at your doorstep equipped with calibrated, certified digital weighing scales.

3
Instant Payout

Actual weight is locked into the app. Total payout is calculated automatically and sent directly via UPI or cash with an official receipt.

4
Circular Processing

Collected materials are aggregated and transferred to certified recycling companies for industrial repurposing into new products.
